# Support

## General Questions

- **GitHub Discussions** (flagship repository): [github.com/Celo-HaiTi/CeloHT/discussions](https://github.com/Celo-HaiTi/CeloHT/discussions)
- **Website bugs**: open an [Issue](https://github.com/Celo-HaiTi/celoht-siteweb/issues) in this repository

## Contact

- General questions: hello@celoht.com
- Security reports (private): security@celoht.com  -  see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)

## Response Time

We aim for 3–5 business days on general questions and 48 hours on
security reports.

## Also See

The in-site [`/support`](https://celoht.com/support) and
[`/faq`](https://celoht.com/faq) pages cover the same information for
end users and community participants.
