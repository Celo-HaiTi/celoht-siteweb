# Governance

CeloHT is governed through transparent, documented, community-driven
proposals and collective decision-making. This document is the canonical
source for governance in this repository and reflects the governing
principle that no single individual may unilaterally establish,
approve, or represent a proposal as an official CeloHT decision.

## Canonical governance principle

- CeloHT is governed through transparent, documented, community-driven
  proposals and collective decision-making.
- A proposal submitted by one person is not automatically an official
  CeloHT decision.
- An individual may propose an idea, initiate a proposal, and participate
  in deliberation, but no single person may unilaterally turn that
  proposal into an official CeloHT decision.
- Founder status is separate from ongoing governance authority. Johnny
  Dubic is permanently recognized as the Founder of CeloHT in the project's
  historical and institutional record.
- Permanent founder recognition is historical and institutional; it does not
  confer perpetual governance authority, ownership rights, veto power, or
  unilateral control.

## Proposal and decision distinction

The governance lifecycle is:

IDEA -> PROPOSAL -> PUBLIC REVIEW / DELIBERATION -> COLLECTIVE DECISION
PROCESS -> APPROVAL / REJECTION -> DOCUMENTED OUTCOME -> IMPLEMENTATION

A proposal must not be described as an approved decision before the
required governance process has occurred. A person submitting a proposal
must not be described as having already decided the matter.

## Who may participate

Any participant may contribute ideas and proposals according to the
published process. Participation is open to contributors, maintainers,
working-group participants, and community members who follow the agreed
review and deliberation process.

## Submission and publication

1. A participant may submit a proposal in the designated public governance
   channel or repository process.
2. The proposal must be documented in a public, reviewable location.
3. The proposal must remain distinct from a final decision until the
   collective review and approval process has been completed.
4. Final decisions must be recorded in a public, auditable outcome note or
   equivalent governance record.

## Review, deliberation, and approval

- Community review occurs in public discussion before any final decision is
  made.
- Deliberation is conducted through the documented governance process.
- Approval or rejection is collective and must be based on the established
  rules for that proposal.
- If a threshold or process requirement has not yet been formally
  established, the status is "To Be Established" rather than invented.
- Final approval is not the same as proposal submission; the proposal may
  be debated, revised, or rejected before becoming an official outcome.

## Governance Council and founder status

- The Founder is Johnny Dubic — Permanently Recognized Founder of CeloHT.
- Founder status is a historical and institutional designation; it does
  not automatically create a Governance Council seat, vote, veto,
  emergency override, or unilateral authority.
- If a Governance Council is formally constituted in the future, its
  authority must come from the documented governance framework, and no
  individual member may claim authority beyond that framework.
- A Council decision requires the documented collective process; no single
  Council member can represent a final decision by personal fiat.
- If the Council has not yet been formally constituted, it is treated as
  pending formation rather than assumed or named.

## Founder responsibilities

The Founder may participate in governance by proposing ideas, helping
provide historical context, contributing technical or institutional
knowledge, and participating in community discussion. However, Founder
status does not create a special approval pathway, veto, override, or
emergency power.

## No token governance

CeloHT does not have a native governance token and does not use
 token-weighted governance. Governance is not based on token ownership,
 token-weighted voting power, or a native CeloHT governance token.

## Conflict of interest and documentation

- Proposals and decisions must be distinguishable in public records.
- Conflicts of interest should be disclosed when relevant to a proposal or
  decision.
- Final outcomes must be documented, reviewable, and auditable.
- Approved decisions must be implemented through the recorded governance
  outcome, not by informal personal declaration.

## Verification status

This repository does not claim a formal institutional verification record
that exceeds the public documentation present in the CeloHT ecosystem.
The founder designation is recognized in public CeloHT historical and
organizational materials, but the repository uses the status
"Pending Verification" when an evidence record is not independently
established in the canonical governance record.

## This repository

This repository follows the shared CeloHT governance model. Content,
documentation, and public messaging changes should preserve the distinction
between proposal, deliberation, and final decision. Any updates to public
CeloHT governance statements should align with this document and should not
imply unilateral founder control.
