# Why Education Comes First

Handing someone a financial tool without the education to go with it isn't opportunity  -  it's risk. That's the belief behind CeloHT's first and most important design decision: every program starts with a classroom, not an app.

## The pattern we're correcting

Financial inclusion efforts often fail at the last mile. Not because the technology doesn't work, but because it arrives without the trust, language, and understanding a community needs to use it safely. We've seen this pattern play out in Haiti before  -  projects built by outsiders, rarely explained in Haitian Creole, and almost never accompanied by a real, sustained education effort.

## What "education first" actually means in practice

- No one gets a Valora wallet demo before completing our basic financial literacy module.
- Blockchain and Web3 fundamentals are taught in plain language, without unexplained jargon.
- Seed-phrase security is covered explicitly, before a participant's first live transaction, not after.
- Risk awareness  -  volatility, common scams  -  is reinforced on an ongoing basis, not a one-time warning.

## Why this is slower, and why that's fine

Education-first is a slower rollout than "download the app and go." We think that trade-off is correct. A participant who understands _why_ they're protecting a seed phrase is safer than one who was just told to.
