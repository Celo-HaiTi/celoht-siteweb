# Team

## Founder

Johnny Dubic — Founder of CeloHT

The Founder role is a permanent historical and institutional designation.
It reflects the founding history of CeloHT and the mission origins of the
initiative. Founder status does not automatically create CEO, Director,
Manager, or Executive Director authority.

The Founder may participate in the governance process by proposing ideas,
providing historical context, contributing technical or institutional
knowledge, and participating in public discussion. However, the Founder does
not have automatic Governance Council membership, a reserved seat, a vote,
a veto, an emergency override, or unilateral decision authority.

Founder responsibilities center on:

- founding history
- mission origins
- institutional continuity
- historical context
- participation in community and governance processes

No founder role in this document implies unilateral authority over the
community or its decisions.
