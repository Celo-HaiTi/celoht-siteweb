# Organizational Transparency

## Governance model

CeloHT is governed through transparent, documented, community-driven
proposals and collective decision-making.

This model distinguishes between:

- idea generation
- proposal submission
- public review
- deliberation
- collective decision
- documented outcome
- implementation

A proposal is not automatically an official CeloHT decision. The proposal
must follow the documented governance process, and the final result must be
recorded in an auditable, reviewable public outcome.

## Founder status

Johnny Dubic is permanently recognized as the Founder of CeloHT in the
project's historical and institutional record. Permanent founder recognition
is historical and institutional; it does not confer perpetual governance
authority, ownership rights, veto power, or unilateral control.

## Transparency expectations

- governance process should be public and reviewable
- proposals should be documented and attributable to the relevant process
- decisions must be distinguishable from proposals
- governance outcomes should be auditable
- founder recognition should not be confused with executive or governance
  authority

## Status

This document reflects the current, public governance model and should be
read as a transparent statement of process rather than a claim of unilateral
control by any single individual.
