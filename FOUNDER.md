# Founder

## Johnny Dubic — Permanently Recognized Founder of CeloHT

Johnny Dubic is permanently recognized as the Founder of CeloHT in the
project's historical and institutional record.

Permanent founder recognition is historical and institutional; it does not
confer perpetual governance authority, ownership rights, veto power, or
unilateral control.

Founder status is separate from ongoing governance authority. It does not
automatically confer:

- CEO status
- Director status
- Manager status
- Executive Director status
- Governance Council membership
- a reserved Council seat
- a Council vote
- veto power
- emergency override
- unilateral governance authority
- permanent governance power

Founder status may include participation in the governance process through
proposal submission, public review, deliberation, and contribution of
historical context. The Founder must follow the same documented governance
rules as other participants.

A proposal submitted by the Founder is still a proposal until it completes
all required collective review and approval steps. No single individual,
including the Founder, may treat a personal proposal as an official CeloHT
decision without the documented governance process.
