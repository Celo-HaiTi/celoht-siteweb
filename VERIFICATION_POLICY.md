# Verification Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how founder and governance roles are verified in CeloHT
public documentation. Verification must be evidence-based, objective,
auditable, transparent, and reproducible.

## Status terms

- Verified: the role or designation is supported by sufficient public,
  objective, reproducible evidence.
- Pending Verification: the documentation and evidence are incomplete,
  inconsistent, or not yet independently established.

## Accepted evidence

Acceptable evidence may include:

- official CeloHT documentation
- official repository references
- consistent public identity records
- historical founding evidence
- legitimate public profiles
- documented governance records

## Rules

- Never fabricate evidence.
- Never create fake institutional confirmation.
- Do not claim governance authority that is not documented.
- Keep founder recognition separate from executive or governance authority.
- If the evidence is not sufficient, use Pending Verification.

## Founder verification

Johnny Dubic is recognized as Founder of CeloHT in public CeloHT materials
and in the project history. This designation is treated as Verified when it
is consistently documented and not contradicted by the current governance
standards.

Founder status does not automatically create executive authority, voting
rights, Council membership, veto rights, or unilateral control.

## Governance verification

Governance claims must be supported by public records and the documented
collective process. Individual proposals are not official CeloHT decisions
until the documented governance process is completed and the outcome is
publicly recorded.
